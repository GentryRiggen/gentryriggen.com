# Judge rubric

Before each check-in, Claude writes `assets/out/<part>/judge.md`: score each
line 1-5 from the reference images and the in-game screenshots, with one
sentence of evidence per line.

1. **Proportions vs refs.** Height to width, taper, cap and band positions.
2. **Silhouette details.** Rim, bands, pipe, base: the shapes visible against the sky.
3. **Surface detail.** Bevels, seams, rivets, anything that catches light.
4. **Finish and style fit.** Colour, gloss and level of detail next to the neighbouring procedural parts.
5. **Reads at game camera distance.** Squint at the in-game shot at its normal size: does it still look right?

End with a one-line verdict and the single change that would help most.
